# Origin of the ADE Human-Machine Framework

## The Architect

The **ADE Human-Machine Framework** was conceived and architected by **Arshad Darius Ebrahim**.

Ebrahim established the original vision, conceptual foundation, architectural model, principles, terminology, and initial direction of the ADE framework.

The framework began as an architectural effort to establish a common foundation for **human and machine understanding without requiring conversion or translation between fundamentally different representations of meaning**.

## Open Development

The role of Architect identifies the origin of the framework and its foundational architecture.

It does not represent a claim of ownership or exclusive control over the framework, nor does it restrict others from contributing to, extending, implementing, or further developing the standards.

ADE is intended to evolve through open participation and collaborative development.
